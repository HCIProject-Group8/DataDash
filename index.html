Interface learning study

A UT Dallas CS 6326 course project on how AI assistants help people learn unfamiliar software.

You'll explore a practice sales dashboard and complete activities involving filtering information, working with charts, and exporting a visualization. You are not expected to know DataDash beforehand.

First you'll answer five short background questions, then receive a dashboard tour and complete an unscored training activity. After that, you'll work through two study tasks. You can ask the AI assistant for help, or work independently. After each study task you'll rate your experience (five short questions), followed by final written feedback.

Plan for approximately 15–25 minutes; actual time may vary. Please use a laptop or desktop and finish in one sitting.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing this page. We do not request your name, email, or exact date of birth. An anonymous study ID will be linked to your optional background answers, task interactions, assistant messages, ratings, and final feedback, and submitted to the research team's study storage. Please avoid entering identifying information in free-text answers.
